Getting started

How do I adjust an app’s volume?

Click the SplitAudio icon in the menu bar. Apps that are playing sound appear under Now Playing; drag the slider, click the speaker to mute, or pick an output device.

macOS asked about System Audio Recording. Why?

macOS requires this permission before any app can change another app’s audio. Choose Allow. SplitAudio processes audio in memory only and never records or uploads it.

The volume controls don’t do anything.
  • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and make sure SplitAudio is turned on.
  • Quit and reopen SplitAudio, then play audio in the app you want to control.
  • Some protected content cannot be captured by macOS and can’t be adjusted.
Why can’t I control Music, Safari or other Apple apps?

SplitAudio intentionally leaves Apple’s system apps and processes untouched.

How do I restore an app’s original sound?

Set it back to 100% with automatic output, or quit SplitAudio. All apps return to their normal playback when SplitAudio quits.

Scenes & Shortcuts

What does a scene do?

A scene saves volume, mute and output for a set of apps. Click it to apply; click it again to turn it off, which returns those apps to how they were before. Changes you make while a scene is on aren’t saved to the scene.

Can I use scenes in Shortcuts?

Yes. In the Shortcuts app, add SplitAudio’s “Apply Scene” action and choose a scene.

Trial & purchases

Is there a free trial?

Yes, all features are free for 7 days with no automatic charge. Afterwards, choose a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

I bought SplitAudio but it’s locked.

Open SplitAudio Settings and click Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage. Or use the Manage Subscription button in SplitAudio Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
