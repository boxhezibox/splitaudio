SplitAudio does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs. Audio is processed only in your Mac’s memory, in real time, and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Who we are

SplitAudio is a macOS menu bar app for controlling the volume, mute state and output device of individual apps. This policy explains how the app handles information. Questions can be sent to heziboite@outlook.com.

Audio and permissions

  • System Audio Recording. To change an app’s volume or output, macOS requires you to allow SplitAudio to access that app’s audio. SplitAudio uses it only for the apps you adjust, changes the level or destination in memory, and plays the result back immediately. Audio is never written to disk, uploaded or analysed for content.
  • Microphone input level. SplitAudio can change the input volume of your microphone device. It does not capture or listen to microphone audio.
  • Choosing an app for a scene. When you pick an application in the file chooser, SplitAudio reads only its name, identifier and icon, with read-only access.

You can review or revoke these permissions at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Information kept on your Mac

To work, SplitAudio saves a few settings locally on your Mac. They never leave your Mac and are not accessible to us:

  • Per-app volume, mute and output device settings, and your scenes.
  • A short “Recently Played” list (app names and identifiers, at most three, removed after 24 hours).
  • Panel and launch-at-login preferences.
  • Your purchase and trial status, kept in your Mac’s keychain so the app keeps working offline. It contains no personal information.

You can clear recent history and reset settings in SplitAudio Settings. Deleting the app removes these settings; the small purchase-status entry may remain in the keychain so a reinstall keeps working.

Purchases

The free trial, subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or address. SplitAudio checks Apple-signed purchase records on your Mac to unlock its features. Apple’s handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy. Subscriptions are governed by Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement.

Network, Shortcuts and third parties

SplitAudio does not connect to our servers or any third-party service. The Shortcuts actions run locally on your Mac. Links in the app, such as this page or the support email, open in your browser or mail app.

Children

SplitAudio does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For any privacy question or request, email heziboite@outlook.com.
